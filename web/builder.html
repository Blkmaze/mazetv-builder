What this page does under the hood

On "Build APK" it POSTs to /repos/<owner>/<repo>/actions/workflows/build-apk.yml/dispatches with your field values as inputs (same inputs the Actions "Run workflow" form takes), then polls /actions/runs for the newest matching run until it finishes, then reads the matching GitHub Release (tag build-<run number>) to find the signed APK's direct download URL. No server, no database, no third party — just your browser and GitHub's API.

Personal build tool for your own MazeTV APKs — not a hosted service.
